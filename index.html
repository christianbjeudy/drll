01 / ABOUT

Care informed by movement & purpose.

Dr. Laurenie Louissaint is double board-certified in Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation (PM&R) and Sports Medicine. Her practice centers on sports medicine and interventional spine care, informed by her own path from Division I athlete and athletic trainer at Florida International University to physician and national-team doctor. She serves as Assistant Professor of PM&R at SUNY Downstate Health Sciences University and as Head Team Physician for the Haitian Football Federation’s Women’s National Team.

CLINICAL APPROACH

Her work is centered on helping patients improve performance, recover from musculoskeletal conditions, and return to the activities that matter to them.

ACADEMIC MEDICINE

At SUNY Downstate, she combines clinical care with teaching, mentorship, and scholarly activity, working with medical students, residents, and fellows in sports and rehabilitation medicine.

AREAS OF EXPERTISE

Specialized care for active lives.

Professional expertise reflected through Dr. Louissaint’s academic & clinical experience.

01Sports MedicineNon-operative care supporting movement, recovery, and performance.+

Clinical interests include sports and overuse injuries, return-to-activity planning, and care organized around each patient’s movement goals.

  • Evaluation of sports, overuse, and activity-related concerns
  • Individualized rehabilitation and return-to-activity planning
  • Collaboration with therapists, trainers, and care teams

This approach connects careful diagnosis with practical recovery planning so active patients can move forward with confidence and clear next steps.

02Physical Medicine & RehabilitationFunction-focused care informed by rehabilitation medicine.+

A function-first approach to conditions affecting muscles, joints, nerves, and mobility, with an emphasis on independence and quality of life.

  • Assessment of muscles, joints, nerves, and mobility
  • Rehabilitation strategies designed around daily and athletic goals
  • Emphasis on independence, participation, and quality of life

Care is framed around the whole person, connecting medical findings to the movements, responsibilities, and activities that shape everyday life.

03Pain Management & Interventional SpineEvidence-informed spine and musculoskeletal pain care with image-guided options.+

Fellowship-informed evaluation of spine-related and musculoskeletal pain using rehabilitation, procedural, and lifestyle strategies.

  • Assessment of spine-related pain, movement, and function
  • Non-operative rehabilitation and recovery planning
  • Selected image-guided procedures when clinically appropriate

A detailed history and examination connect symptoms with function before a balanced, individualized treatment plan is considered.

04Women’s Musculoskeletal HealthEvidence-based sports medicine care for women across the lifespan.+

Comprehensive sports medicine care supporting women’s physical health, activity, recovery, and athletic performance across the lifespan.

  • Injury prevention, diagnosis, and non-surgical treatment
  • Concussion and musculoskeletal injury management
  • Pain-management options and performance-focused guidance

Care is tailored to each patient’s stage of life, movement goals, and athletic demands, with an emphasis on informed decisions and sustainable performance.

05Musculoskeletal UltrasoundUltrasound-supported assessment and procedural care.+

Real-time ultrasound may support soft-tissue and joint evaluation and help guide selected procedures with precision.

  • Real-time soft-tissue and joint visualization
  • Dynamic evaluation when movement matters
  • Guidance for selected procedures when clinically appropriate

Because ultrasound can show structures in motion, it can add useful context to an examination and support precise, patient-centered decision-making.

06Cycling & Running MedicineMovement-focused care for endurance athletes and active patients.+

Care for endurance athletes and active patients may include training-related concerns, biomechanics, recovery, and safe return to sport.

  • Training-related and overuse concerns
  • Biomechanics, recovery, and training-load considerations
  • Safe return to endurance activity and sport

Her perspective as a cyclist complements clinical training with an appreciation for endurance, consistency, equipment, and the realities of returning to sport.

ATHLETE · 2005–2010

Dr. Louissaint competed in Division I track and field at FIU, with events spanning hurdles, high jump, long jump, and sprint competition. That experience built an early understanding of training demands, performance, injury, and recovery from the athlete’s point of view.

•Athletic Trainer to PhysicianBuilt a foundation in athletic training before pursuing medical school and sports medicine.+
ATHLETIC TRAINING · MEDICINE

Her Master of Arts in Athletic Training at FIU and work as an athletic and personal trainer created a direct bridge from performance and rehabilitation into medicine. Today, that foundation shapes a practical approach to sports medicine and interventional spine care.

•Field-Side & Event MedicineSupports athletes in the environments where they train and compete.+

Her event-medicine experience includes field-side support for youth and school athletics in New York City, medical coverage for boxing competition, and caring for runners at the New York City Marathon.
